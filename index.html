    Open data & sources

    Longleaf combines public agency data, community contributions, and third-party map services. Sources include USGS, NOAA/NWS, USACE, state wildlife agencies, local governments, and © OpenStreetMap contributors (ODbL). Source licenses and map attributions apply. No agency endorsement is implied.

    Use with care

    Information is provided “as is” for general recreation planning and may be incomplete, inaccurate, or outdated. Verify access, property boundaries, permits, fishing rules, weather, water levels, and dam releases with official sources before heading out. A mapped location does not grant permission to enter or confirm safe conditions.
