Longleaf Atlas map badges

Maki CC0 glyphs: campsite, dam, harbor, parking, water, marker, toilet, triangle. Source: https://github.com/mapbox/maki (retrieved 2026-10-26). The restroom and structure badges use the Maki toilet and marker glyphs; their source SVGs are retained as `maki-toilet-source.svg` and `maki-marker-source.svg`. License: MAKI-LICENSE.txt.

Boat ramp, paddle launch, fish, hiking and gauge glyphs and badge styling: Longleaf Atlas. Icons are bundled locally for offline use. Colors distinguish feature categories, not safety or legal access.

The habitat badge uses the Maki CC0 wetland glyph, retrieved 2026-10-27 from https://github.com/mapbox/maki. Its unmodified source is retained as `maki-wetland-source.svg`. Habitat green distinguishes historical vegetation surveys from the rose structure/reef symbol; neither indicates safe water or current ecological condition.
